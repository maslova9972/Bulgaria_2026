# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React with Vite. Static deployment to GitHub Pages. The build must use repository-safe relative asset paths and require no server runtime.

## Users

Russian-speaking entrepreneurs, experts, investors, and expatriates living across Europe who want a useful professional circle and a combined business and travel experience.

## Product Purpose

Present and sell participation in Business & Travel Bulgaria 2026, a six-day international business trip at Sunny Beach from 8 to 13 September 2026. The page should help visitors understand the people, programme, setting, and participation options, then start a conversation with organiser Natalia Maslova in Telegram.

## Positioning

The event combines a working business forum, structured international networking, and a curated coastal programme in one trip. Visitors do not have to choose between professional development and a restorative holiday.

## Operating Context

Most visitors will arrive from social media on mobile. They need to assess the event quickly, recognise relevant experts, understand what happens on 12 September, compare accommodation options, and contact the organiser through an external messenger.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One-page Russian-language marketing site.
- Static hosting on GitHub Pages.
- Preserve the event dates, location, speaker identities, programme facts, pricing, and organiser contact destinations from the supplied reference.
- Primary conversion: organiser Natalia Maslova in Telegram, `@maslovanataly`, `https://t.me/maslovanataly`.
- Keep an empty, non-visible embed slot in the final contact section for a future sales-funnel form.
- The page must be responsive, keyboard accessible, motion-safe, and usable without a backend.
- A countdown must resolve gracefully after the event rather than displaying misleading zero values.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Business & Travel Bulgaria 2026.
- Language: Russian, with participant quotes retained in their original language where factual.
- The redesign should feel materially more editorial and premium than the reference, with Awwwards-level typography and composition.
- The Black Sea setting, international character, and human connection are central brand signals.

## Evidence on Hand

- Public reference page: `https://bulgaria-business-sea.lovable.app/`.
- Confirmed event copy, dates, programme, prices, speaker biographies, participant quotes, public portraits, and destination photographs are present on the reference page.
- Pricing: 500 EUR for one person, 700 EUR for two people, and 800 EUR for three people. Flights and transfer are excluded.
- No additional analytics contract, legal copy, booking backend, or private brand asset package was provided. Do not fabricate endorsements, availability, or new commercial claims.

## Product Principles

1. Make the value of the combined business and travel format clear in the first viewport.
2. Put real people and a credible programme ahead of generic lifestyle promises.
3. Keep the mobile path from interest to messenger contact short and obvious.
4. Use premium visual craft to increase trust without obscuring practical details.
5. Preserve factual content while reducing repetition and reading fatigue.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast, provide visible keyboard focus, semantic landmarks, meaningful image descriptions, reduced-motion support, and touch targets suitable for mobile visitors.
